# Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 — effective 7 November 2026**

AlmaNav is a free hobby project made by a private individual, Aliaksandr Piatrou ("the author", "I"). This policy says
what data the App uses, where it goes and what you can do about it.

## The short version

- The App has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It has no server of its own.
- **The author receives no data from the App**: not your location, routes, searches or settings.
- Your location is used on your phone to navigate and is not sent anywhere by the App.
- A few features fetch data from third-party services. Those services see your IP address and what is listed below.

## What stays on your phone

- **Location.** GPS position, also in the background while a ride is on, to follow the route and time the voice cues.
  It is not stored as a history and not sent anywhere.
- **Routes, profiles and settings**, including when you accepted these notices and the bend-notes acknowledgement.
  They are kept in the App's storage on your phone. They may be included in your phone's own iCloud or computer
  backup, under Apple's terms, if you have backups switched on.
- **Map packs** you download, and routing and voice guidance, which run entirely on the phone.

## What goes to third parties, and when

- **Photon, by komoot GmbH** (photon.komoot.io), when you search for a place online: the search text and an
  approximate location to rank the results.
- **Nominatim, by the OpenStreetMap Foundation** (nominatim.openstreetmap.org), when you open a place's details: the
  OpenStreetMap ID of that place.
- **OpenFreeMap** (openfreemap.org), when the map shows an area outside your downloaded packs: requests for the map
  tiles of that area.
- **Cloudflare R2**, which hosts the map packs, when you download or update a pack or the App checks for updates:
  which pack or pack list is requested.

Each of these services also sees your IP address and basic technical data of the request, as any website does, and
handles it under its own privacy policy. The App sends them no identifier of you or your phone.

Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with developers if you allowed this in your phone's settings
(Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements). That is controlled by you and Apple, not by the App.

## Legal basis (EU and Cyprus)

The author does not collect or process your personal data, so the author is not a controller of it under the GDPR.
Data sent to the services above is sent at your request, to provide the feature you use; each service is responsible
for what it does with it.

## Your choices

- Location access can be changed or withdrawn at any time in Settings › AlmaNav › Location. Navigation needs it.
- Not using online search, place details or areas outside your packs avoids the related requests.
- Deleting the App deletes everything it stored on your phone.

## Children

The App is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date are shown at the top and in the App.
