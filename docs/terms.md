## 1. Safety first — the short version

- **You are riding, not the App.** Always watch the road. Road signs, markings, traffic lights, the police and
  traffic law always take precedence over the App.
- **The App can be wrong.** Maps, routes, voice cues, distances, lanes, landmarks, speed bumps, gradients, road
  surfaces and restricted zones may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.
- **Never follow an instruction that is unsafe, illegal or impossible.** If the App tells you to turn where you
  cannot or should not, do not turn. Carry on and let it recalculate.
- **Do not handle the phone while riding.** Set the route before you set off. Stop safely before touching the phone.
- **Keep the volume so you can still hear traffic**, and use a headset only where the law allows it.

## 2. What the App is

The App is a **navigation aid**. It suggests routes and speaks guidance computed automatically, on your phone, from
OpenStreetMap data — a free map edited by volunteers. Nobody, including the author, checks the routes or cues against
the real roads. The App is not a certified navigation system and is not suitable as the only means of navigation.

## 3. Map data and routes

OpenStreetMap data, and anything derived from it, can contain errors and omissions, and roads change faster than maps.
In particular, a suggested route may:

- use unpaved, dirt or gravel roads, tracks, farm roads or roads in poor condition, or roads unsuitable for your
  motorcycle, your skill, the weather or the time of day;
- include steep gradients, hairpins, narrow or unlit mountain roads (for example in the Troodos), fords, or sections
  with no mobile signal;
- use roads that are closed, private, one-way, under construction, seasonally closed, or where your vehicle is not
  allowed;
- not reflect traffic, accidents, events, police roadblocks or temporary restrictions.

You are responsible for choosing a route, for judging whether every part of it is safe and legal for you, and for
following it. Profile settings such as "dirt", "highways" or "twistiness" change which roads the router prefers; they
do not make any road safe.

## 4. Voice cues, bend notes and other guidance

Voice cues are generated by rules from the same map data and your phone's GPS position. GPS may be inaccurate,
delayed or lost (tunnels, valleys, buildings, phone settings, battery saving). A cue may therefore come late, early,
refer to the wrong junction or landmark, or not come at all, and the phone or headset may lose audio.

**Bend notes** (rally-style pace notes, an experimental feature, off by default, in Settings › Experimental) describe bends calculated from the
map's road shape. They are not measured from the real road. **A grade may understate how tight a bend really is**,
and a bend may be missed or called where there is none. The notes do not account for descent, crests, blind entries,
road surface, camber, visibility, gravel, oncoming traffic or other conditions, and are **not an invitation to ride
fast**. AlmaNav is not a racing app. The safe speed for a bend depends on your skill, your motorcycle, tyres, load,
weather and road conditions, none of which the App knows. Never choose your speed by a bend note: judge every bend by
what you see. Always obey speed limits and ride at a speed at which you can stop within the distance you can see.

Speed shown by the App is from GPS and may differ from your real speed; rely on your motorcycle's speedometer.
Warnings such as steep climbs, speed bumps or gravel may be missing where they exist and present where they do not.

## 5. Borders, the buffer zone and restricted areas (Cyprus)

The App tries to avoid the UN buffer zone and other restricted or military zones using map data, but that data may be
incomplete or wrong, and the App may not warn you. Never enter the buffer zone except at an official crossing point,
and never enter military or other restricted areas. Before crossing to the north, check the rules that apply to you,
including vehicle insurance (insurance from the Republic of Cyprus is generally not valid there) and any rental
agreement. The same care applies at any border in any country covered by a map pack.

services (Photon by komoot, Nominatim by the OpenStreetMap Foundation) and may be wrong or out of date. Check
anything important yourself.
